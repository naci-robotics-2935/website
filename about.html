Who We Are

Our
Mission

At NACI Robotics, our mission is to empower students through hands-on STEM experiences that foster creativity, collaboration, and leadership.

We are committed to making science, technology, engineering, and math more accessible and inclusive for all, while building innovative solutions, inspiring our community, and breaking barriers through equity, excellence, and resilience.

Lab
Safety

NACI Robotics adheres to the policies of the NACI Robotics lab safety contract’s policies, and makes sure all members and mentors are educated and aware of how it applies to them. This contract addresses both lab safety rules and team member behavior. Once signed, members and mentors agree to follow the established guidelines to maintain a safe, respectful, and productive environment.
