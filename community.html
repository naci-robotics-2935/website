Founded in 2009, 2935 NACI Robotics is a proud FIRST Robotics team representing North Albion Collegiate Institute.

Our team brings together passionate students who design, build, and program competitive robots while developing real-world engineering, teamwork, and leadership skills. We strive to inspire innovation, creativity, and community through the spirit of STEM and collaboration.
