We Don’t Just Build Robots

NACI Robotics is a passionate high school robotics team from Rexdale, Ontario, with 30+ members. We compete in FIRST, a global robotics competition, and are dedicated to learning, building, and inspiring through STEM. Beyond competing, we run youth workshops, school demos, and mentor other teams to make STEM accessible to all.

About FIRST

What FIRST is

Learn more about FRC

FIRST, For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology, is a global organization that uses robotics competitions to inspire young people to explore STEM (science, technology, engineering, and math). Every year, students from around the world work together to design, build, and program robots to compete in high energy challenges. But it’s not just about robots. FIRST is about teamwork, problem-solving, and innovation. We’re proud to be part of the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC), the highest level of competition for high school students. It’s where students take on real engineering challenges, build advanced robots, and create memories and skills that last beyond the season, with FIRST having a proven impact on STEM learning, interest, and skill-building well beyond high school.
