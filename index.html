Who
We Are

Founded in 2009, 2935 NACI Robotics is a proud FIRST Robotics team representing North Albion Collegiate Institute. Our team brings together passionate students who design, build, and program competitive robots while developing real-world engineering, teamwork, and leadership skills.

Beyond competing, we run youth workshops, school demos, and mentor other teams to make STEM accessible to all.

What
Is FIRST

firstinspires.org

FIRST, For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology, is a global organization that uses robotics competitions to inspire young people to explore STEM. Every year, students from around the world work together to design, build, and program robots to compete in high energy challenges.

But it’s not just about robots. FIRST is about teamwork, problem-solving, and innovation. We’re proud to be part of the FIRST Robotics Competition, the highest level of competition for high school students, where students take on real engineering challenges and build skills that last well beyond the season.
