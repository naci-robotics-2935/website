Our Programs

What we run when we are not building our own robot.

HackNights

Every week

What are HackNights?

HackNights are fun, hands-on build events where students team up to tackle creative engineering challenges. Each week brings a brand new activity, from building spaghetti towers to designing rubber band powered cars or crafting parachutes. Students work together to brainstorm, plan, and build, developing problem-solving, teamwork, and communication skills in a supportive, interactive environment.

Learning through doing

HackNights help students build a strong foundation in engineering, design, and critical thinking while fostering creativity and curiosity. By practicing problem-solving every week, students gain confidence and develop skills that prepare them for bigger challenges like Minibots, FLL, and eventually FRC.

FIRST LEGO League

Ages 9 to 16

FIRST LEGO League (FLL) is a robotics competition for students, usually ages 9 to 16. Teams build and program LEGO robots to complete missions on a themed field, and they also work on an Innovation Project to solve a real-world problem. The game is fully autonomous, meaning the bot runs on its own, which is why consistency is key.

STEM and technical skills

  • Programming: code LEGO robots using LEGO Spike or EV3
  • Engineering and design: design, build, and test robots
  • Problem solving: make your robot do tasks efficiently
  • Critical thinking: improve the design each time it fails

Soft skills

  • Teamwork: work with others to share ideas
  • Communication: explain your ideas to judges clearly
  • Creativity: invent new solutions for real problems
  • Time management: plan, test, and present before the day
  • Resilience: keep going even when your robot fails

MiniBots

Ages 14 to 18  /  2 vs 2

What are MiniBots?

The MiniBot Competition is an exciting multi-week robotics program that challenges high school students aged 14 to 18 to design, build, and compete with their own custom MiniBots. Modeled after the FIRST Robotics Competition, it combines creativity, strategy, and engineering in dynamic 2 vs 2 matches.

The focus

Each MiniBot is designed and produced by Lord of Robots, a company focused on making robotics education simple, fun, and accessible for everyone. These MiniBots are engineered to be both powerful and easy to build, using durable components and intuitive controls that allow students to start building right away.

FIRST Robotics Competition Team 2935 at North Albion Collegiate Institute, Rexdale, Ontario. Competing since 2009.
