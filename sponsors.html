The People Behind Us

NACI Robotics thanks its sponsors for their important and continuous support to keep the team running.

Lead partner

The Rexdale Community Hub

The Rexdale Community Hub is our primary sponsor and our biggest supporter. The Hub’s Makerspace has streamlined our robot fabrication, and the 3D printing resources they make accessible to our students has proven invaluable to our design and build process, contributing significantly to our success this year.

Technical mentors & equipment

Endava

Endava’s generous donation allowed us to buy parts, tools and other equipment. We are also fortunate to have two technical mentors from Endava who have spent much of their time teaching students strategy, programming, CAD and 3D printing.

World Championship travel

Rexdale Community Health Centre

The Rexdale Community Health Centre generously helped our team by covering many of the expenses during our stay in Houston, Texas for the FIRST World Championship.

Registration & travel

Toronto District School Board

One of our longest-standing sponsors, the TDSB has helped our team by paying for registration and travel costs to the FIRST Robotics World Championship.

Parts & manufacturing

Rotary Club of Etobicoke

The Rotary Club of Etobicoke supports the team through monetary support that helps us buy the parts needed for the robot, along with tools and supplies for manufacturing.

Workshops & MiniBots

Lord of Robots

Lord of Robots educates our students with skill workshops such as 3D printing, encourages problem solving through the MiniBot programme, and helps transport our robot to and from competitions.
