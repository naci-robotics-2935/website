Our sponsors

The People Behind Us

NACI Robotics thanks its sponsors for their important and continuous support to keep the team running.

The Rexdale Community Hub

The Rexdale Community Hub is our primary sponsor and our biggest supporter. The Hub’s Makerspace has streamlined our robot fabrication and the 3D printing resources that they make accessible to our students has proven to be invaluable to our design and robot build process, contributing significantly to our success this year.

Endava

Endava’s generous donation allowed us to buy parts, tools and other equipment for our robot. Additionally, we are also fortunate to have 2 technical mentors from Endava that have spent much of their time and hard work teaching students skills, strategy, programming, CAD, 3D printing and more.

The Rexdale Community Health Centre

The Rexdale Community Health Centre has generously helped our team this season by covering many expenses during our stay in Houston, Texas for the FIRST World Championship.

Toronto District School Board

The Toronto District School Board is one of our longest-standing sponsors and has helped our team by paying for registration and travel costs to the FIRST Robotics World Championship.

Rotary Club of Etobicoke

The Rotary Club of Etobicoke is another generous sponsor that has helped our team through monetary support that helped us buy parts needed for the robot and other tools and supplies for manufacturing.

Lord of Robots

Lord of Robots helped out our team by educating students with skill workshops such as 3D printing, has encouraged problem solving skills through MiniBots, and helped transport our robot to and from competitions.

Back The Team

Sponsorship pays for parts, registration and travel, and it puts engineering tools in the hands of students who would not otherwise have them. If your organization would like to support NACI Robotics, we would like to hear from you.
